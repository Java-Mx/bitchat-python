1. ZERO TELEMETRY, NO ACCOUNTS, NO CENTRAL SERVERS
BitChat is a local-first, peer-to-peer terminal messaging application:
- No Personal Data Collection: BitChat does NOT collect your name, email
  address, phone number, IP address, or personal identity.
- No Analytics or Telemetry: BitChat contains NO analytics SDKs, crash
  reporters, usage tracking, or telemetry transmission code. It makes
  zero outbound connections to publisher or third-party servers.
- No Accounts or Registration: No account or sign-up is required to
  download, install, or use BitChat.
- No Marketing or Tracking Consent: Acknowledging this Privacy Policy
  during setup is informational only and is NEVER treated as consent to
  analytics, tracking, or marketing.
  (Note: internal methods named get_telemetry() in the codebase return
  strictly local, in-memory hardware/transport status for your own TUI
  status bar and /status command; nothing ever leaves your device.)

2. DATA STORED LOCALLY ON YOUR DEVICE
BitChat stores local data in %USERPROFILE%\.bitchat\:
- identity.json: Your locally generated Ed25519 and X25519 private keys,
  fingerprint, and peer ID, restricted to your Windows user account via
  protected Windows file ACLs.
- config.json: Your local display nickname (default "Anonymous"), UI
  theme/density/accent, active transport ("bluetooth" or "lan"), mesh
  hop limit, fragment delay, and keybindings.
- Installer Metadata: Standard per-user uninstall entries under HKCU and
  an optional per-user Startup shortcut (%APPDATA%\Microsoft\Windows\
  Start Menu\Programs\Startup\BitChat.lnk) only if you explicitly enable
  "Start BitChat automatically when Windows starts".

Upgrades and Uninstallation:
- Upgrades preserve %USERPROFILE%\.bitchat\ automatically.
- Uninstallation preserves %USERPROFILE%\.bitchat\ by default so you do
  not lose your private identity keys, while offering an explicit,
  separate option if you wish to permanently delete local user data.

3. DATA SHARED OVER PEER-TO-PEER TRANSPORTS (BLE & LAN)
When you run BitChat with Bluetooth or LAN transport enabled:
- BLE Transport: Broadcasts and scans for the BitChat GATT service UUID
  (6E400001-B5A3-F393-E0A9-E50E24DCCA9E). Nearby BLE peers can observe
  your node's BLE advertisement, nickname, peer ID, and public keys.
- LAN Transport: Broadcasts UDP discovery beacons (port 24024 / 41234)
  and accepts framed TCP connections (port 24025 / 41235) on your local
  subnet. Peers on the same subnet can observe your local IP address,
  nickname, peer ID, and public keys.
- Messages: #public messages are broadcast to reachable mesh nodes.
  Direct messages (/dm) are end-to-end encrypted with Noise XX
  (Noise_XX_25519_ChaChaPoly_SHA256) or AES-256-GCM.
