4. PEER-TO-PEER & MULTI-HOP MESH BEHAVIOR
-----------------------------------------
1. Direct Broadcast & Discovery: Your device broadcasts BLE advertisements
   (Service UUID F47B5E2D-4A9E-4C5A-9B3F-8E1D2C3A4B5C) or LAN UDP discovery
   beacons (default port 41234, including peer_id, nickname, TCP port, and
   detected Wi-Fi SSID) when not in Offline Mode.
2. Multi-Hop Packet Relaying: When max_hops / TTL > 1 (default 3, max 7), your
   node may automatically deduplicate, buffer in memory, and relay packets on
   behalf of nearby peers.
3. Untrusted Remote Peers: Nearby devices can read unencrypted outer packet
   headers and public broadcast messages (0x04 Message). Transmitted messages
   cannot be recalled from remote devices.


5. IDENTITY, KEY MANAGEMENT & SECURITY LIMITATIONS
--------------------------------------------------
- Local Key Ownership: Private keys are stored locally in
  ~/.bitchat/identity.json (%USERPROFILE%\.bitchat\identity.json on Windows)
  without central recovery or passphrase encryption at rest.
- No Independent Security Audit: While BitChat uses established primitives
  (Noise_XX_25519_ChaChaPoly_SHA256, X25519, Ed25519, ChaCha20-Poly1305,
  AES-256-GCM, HKDF-SHA256, PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256), it has NOT undergone an
  independent third-party cryptographic or security audit. Do not rely on
  BitChat for life-safety, emergency, or critical high-threat operations.
- Ephemeral Chat History: Chat messages are held in RAM only during the active
  session and are not saved to disk in v0.3.1.
