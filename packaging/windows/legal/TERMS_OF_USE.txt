3. DESCRIPTION OF SOFTWARE & PEER-TO-PEER OPERATION
BitChat is a decentralized, peer-to-peer terminal messaging application
operating over Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and Local Area Networks (LAN).
- BitChat operates without central servers, user accounts, or cloud
  relays.
- When Bluetooth or LAN transport is active, BitChat discovers and
  communicates directly with nearby peers within radio range or on your
  local network subnet.
- The Windows installer allows you to configure your installation path,
  user PATH integration, shortcuts, and optional Windows sign-in
  startup. BitChat does not install hidden background services.

5. USER RESPONSIBILITIES & KEY MANAGEMENT
- Local Cryptographic Identity: Your private keys are generated and
  stored locally in %USERPROFILE%\.bitchat\identity.json (protected by
  current-user Windows ACLs). There is no central key escrow or recovery
  service. If you delete your identity keys, your cryptographic identity
  cannot be recovered.
