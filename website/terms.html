2. Decentralized Peer-to-Peer Mesh Relay Model

By running BitChat, your instance operates as an autonomous node in a decentralized mesh network:

  • Packet Forwarding: In multi-hop mesh mode, your node may receive and forward encrypted packets destined for other peers within radio or network reach. Your node cannot read the encrypted contents of forwarded packets.
  • Local Resource Use: The application utilizes your device's Bluetooth radio adapter, network interfaces, CPU, and RAM.
  • No Content Moderation: Because BitChat operates without central servers or administrators, the authors and contributors cannot monitor, censor, filter, or moderate messages broadcast over radio or local networks. You are solely responsible for compliance with applicable local telecommunications laws and regulations.

3. Cryptographic Key Ownership & Recovery

Your cryptographic identity (Ed25519 public and private keypair) is generated and stored exclusively on your device in ~/.bitchat/identity.json:

  • There is no central registry, backup server, or password reset mechanism.
  • If you delete or lose access to your local identity key, your peer cryptographic identity cannot be restored by the maintainers or any third party.

4. Disclaimer of Warranties & Operational Risk

BitChat is experimental communication software. The software is provided "AS IS", without warranties of uptime, delivery guarantees, or physical radio range. In particular:

  • Do not rely on BitChat as your sole communication channel during critical life safety emergencies.
  • Radio transmissions over Bluetooth Low Energy and local UDP broadcasts are subject to environmental interference, range limitations, and packet loss.
